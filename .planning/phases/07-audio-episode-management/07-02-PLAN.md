2. **Create `src/hooks/useAudioIntegration.ts`** — hook that connects game events to sounds:
   - Import `audioManager` from `@/lib/audioManager`
   - Import `useShowStore` from `@/state`
   - Uses `useShowStore.subscribe` (Zustand subscribe) to detect state changes and play appropriate sounds:
   
   **Scoring events** — subscribe to score changes:
   - When `rightScore` or `leftScore` increases: play `correct`
   - When `rightScore` or `leftScore` decreases: play `wrong`
   - When score crosses multiples of 50: play `score-milestone` (check both teams)
   
   **Section navigation events** — subscribe to `currentSection` changes:
   - When currentSection changes from null to a value: play `section-enter`
   - When currentSection changes from one section to another: play `section-exit` then after 300ms play `section-enter`
   - Special case: if new section is `'windows'` and `sectionState.isMinefieldQuestion` becomes true: play `minefield-danger`
   
   **Section-specific events** — subscribe to sectionState:
   - `isMinefieldQuestion` transitions to true: play `minefield-danger`
   - `debateRevealedCount` increases: play `debate-reveal`
   
   - The hook returns nothing — it's a side-effect-only hook
   - Call `useAudioIntegration()` once in `OperatorControls.tsx` (operator-only — audience doesn't play audio)
   
   **Minefield scoring** — detect when scoring happens during minefield:
   - If `isMinefieldQuestion` is true and score increased by 16: play `minefield-correct`
   - If `isMinefieldQuestion` is true and score decreased by 8: play `minefield-wrong`
   - (Detect by checking score delta + minefield flag in the subscription)

3. **Wire mute shortcut in OperatorControls.tsx**:
   - Import `useAudio` from `@/hooks/useAudio`
   - Add `useHotkeys('m', () => toggleMute())` for the M key mute toggle (from shortcutRegistry)
   - Call `useAudioIntegration()` in OperatorControls to activate audio event detection
   - Import and call `useAudio()` to get `toggleMute` and `isMuted`
   - Show a small mute indicator icon in the persistent zone header (speaker icon with slash when muted) — use Lucide `Volume2` / `VolumeX` icons
